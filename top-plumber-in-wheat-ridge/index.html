What to share and what the visit may involve

Plumbing work starts with understanding the issue. Depending on the concern, the plumber may need to look at the affected fixture or plumbing equipment and ask when the symptoms occur. The specific work depends on what is found, so avoid assuming that a visible symptom tells the whole story.

Before making the call, use this short checklist:
- Identify the fixture or area where you noticed the problem.
- Note when it started and whether it happens all the time or only sometimes.
- Mention any change in water flow, sound, or visible moisture.
- Keep the path to the affected area clear if you can do so safely.
- Tell the plumber if you have already tried anything to address it.

Avoid taking apart plumbing or applying a product you are unsure about. If the issue involves water near electrical equipment, do not touch the equipment. Explain the situation when you call and follow professional guidance.
